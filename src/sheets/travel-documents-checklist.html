The copies rule

Passports and critical IDs: ONE copy goes in the checked bag, one stays with a contact at home, and the originals never leave the carry-on. Write the copies location here: ________________

Names as they appear

Ticket names need to match ID letter for letter — middle names included on passports. Fix this while booking is cheap, gone is complicated, and check-in day is worse.
