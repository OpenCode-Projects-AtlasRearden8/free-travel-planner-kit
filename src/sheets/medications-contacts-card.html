Before you fold it up

  • Medications travel in original labeled bottles in the carry-on, not in daily organizers, so the dose and the label agree at any counter.
  • Double quantity is hard to buy twice — pack the exact number of trip days plus 4 days of buffer in case of delays.
  • Fold along this line: keep personal rows on the inside, contacts on the outer panel. Write "Property of ___" on the outer panel.
  • Leave a second copy of this card with a trusted person at home. Shred it after the trip.
