At-Home-While-Away Checklist

Trips feel calmer when home behaves itself while you're gone. Two phases: before you walk out, and when you're back.

Before walking out the door

✓TaskWho / notes
Pet sitter / kennel confirmed and booked 
Plants watered; watering drinks for the trip ready 
Trash & recycling out; perishables thrown out 
Water taps and all doors, windows, garage checked 
Thermostat set; water heater set (some heat vs none) 
Mail: hold the mail and hold package deliveries 
Security: lights on timers or smart lights scheduled 
Car: parked, locked, and plugged in if electric 
Valuables hidden & safe; laptop locked away 
Neighbor contact copy: gate codes, alarm notes 
Cards and wallet: on your person, never packed deep 
Final door lock check (twice works every time) 

While you're away — the minimal involvement plan

✓Ask a helperHow often
Take bins to the curb and back 
Quick walk-by so it feels lived-in 
Water anything surprisingly thirsty 
Pick up any shocking deliveries 
Give an emergency contact a slip of keys 

Back-home re-entry (the step everyone skips)

✓TaskNotes
Come home to a calm space — no surprise cleanup before sitting down 
Packages, deliveries, and outside stuff brought in 
Thermostat and water heater back on 
Grab-first laundry, then a real meal: fix the re-entry dinner plan 
Thank the pet sitter / helpers 
Close the trip: shred the meds card copy, end the checklist 

Holdmail tip

A pile of visible mail is the number one giveaway nobody's home. Holdmail services exist in most countries — book them before travel day, and note the start & end dates here: ____ to ____

Dark house fix

Lamps on timers, one evening, one hallway. Mirrored windows in a dark house at 2 a.m. scream "everything's outside." Timers solve two visibility and four presence cues cheaply.
