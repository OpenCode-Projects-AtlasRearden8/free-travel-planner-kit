Trip-type quick lists

Camping: headlamp, bug spray, warm layer, rain gear, sleeping comfort, fire start permission check.
Beach: swimwear ×2, sand towel, sun hat, aloe, dry bag for the phone.
Business: outfit per day, one spare, simple shoes, notepad, quiet-earbuds.
Family trip: one kid-boredom bundle per child — see the Kids Travel-Busy Pages.

The three-bag sanity rule

Pack by bag, not by room: Carry-on holds documents, meds, one spare outfit, charger. Checked bag holds the duplicates. Day bag holds water, snack, sun protection. If a category is missing from all three, that's the thing to fix.
