How to use this sheet

Kid Travel-Busy Pages · paper quiet time, no battery required.

Print a set per child, on purpose

One crayon box, one kid's set — pre-printed sets are fun in the first 20 minutes and life-saving at hour two. Slip each set on a clipboard or a lightweight kitchen cutting board: instant hard writing surface.

Debut them slowly

Hand out one page at a time. Rationed fun runs for hours; fun dumped at once runs for about 18 minutes.

Prizes the grown-ups agree on

Set the scavenger-hunt prize before you leave — extra screen time, snack choice, a 50-cent souvenir for the hunt winner. A trip reward you decided mid-drive never survives negotiation.

Add your own scrambles

Take a marker before you leave home and unscramble in your kids' favorite words — sibling names, the next city, their favorite snacks. Nobody grades these pages, including you.
