How to use this sheet

Packing List Builder · travel readier than last time, without the spreadsheet.

Fill the top strip first

Your trip type, days and weather are better packers than any list. A beach week and a mountain cabin need different boxes checked — the details row down decides that for you.

Pack by category, not by room

Clothes, toiletries, tech, everyday — walk around the house once per category rather than once per room. It's slower-feeling, faster-actually, and you end the session done.

Copies of things beat luck

Prescription meds go in the carry-on in original bottles. Chargers stay behind more than phones. One spare packed where you can reach it means a bad day is only an inconvenience.

Make it yours

Strike any item that never applies to your trips and write your own rows on the blank seams. Printing this every trip is one glance, not a micro-project. Need more blank lines? Copy this page as many times as you need.
