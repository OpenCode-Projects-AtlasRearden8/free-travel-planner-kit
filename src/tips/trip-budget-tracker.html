How to use this sheet

Trip Budget Tracker · guess it, log it, get smarter about it every trip.

Fill Plan A before the trip

Not exact — rough. The "bump" column already adds the classic overshoot for each category, so a modest Plan A guess totals something you can actually trust. If your totals sit well below the bump, expect them to catch up.

Two minutes each evening

Log the day's spend at night and it stays two minutes. A photo of each receipt during the day makes evening matching fast and errant cash moments rare. Skip a day? Guess and mark it — a good-enough log beats a perfect one that stops.

Rounding is allowed

Logging to the nearest dollar (or whole local-currency unit) keeps the whole page legible. Exact numbers cause exact quitting.

Next-trip Plan A fixes itself

After the trip, the "come home" table shows which category was off. Yank that insight into the next trip's Plan A and each trip gets cheaper to plan, calmer to run, and easier to enjoy.
