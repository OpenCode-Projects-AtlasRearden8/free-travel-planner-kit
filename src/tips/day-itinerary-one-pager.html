How to use this sheet

Day Itinerary One-Pager · print one per day, sketch, and let the sketch breathe.

Two or three fixed things is a good day

A trip fills itself around what can't move — the early-entry ticket, the 7 p.m. dinner, the 4-hour train. Put those in first, leave real blank space, and the day breathes rather than collapsing.

Paper beats a dead phone

Write the places' names and addresses on this sheet each morning. When the map app pessimistically dies, the paper still does. This one page keeps the trip going.

Write Plan B on purpose

Rain, closed exhibits, exhausted people and toddlers happen to any trip, any day. Deciding your rain plan before the rain is 80% of the win.

The bottom box becomes your travel journal

A line about what worked and what to skip next time is how each trip makes the next one better. Combined with the budget tracker, you get a complete post-trip review in under five minutes.
