How to use this sheet

Travel Documents Checklist · every paper, card and confirmation, one page.

Do it a week out, not the night before

Expiring passports and missing confirmations are fixable weeks ahead and miserable at a counter. Do a 10-minute papers walk a week out, and again the night before with this sheet as the only thing you trust.

Write in the confirmation numbers

A screenshot that can't load and a confirmation number you can read aloud are different tools. Write the numbers on one sheet — it doesn't need a battery.

The carry-on rule

Documents, meds, and payment cards ride in the carry-on — the checked bag is a temporary stranger, and so is your day bag in any airport lounge. Assume things wander; pack so the important stuff never does.

Copies as backup, never as replacement

A photo of your passport in the camera roll speeds up replacing it; it never replaces it. Email copies to yourself as a second layer.
