How to use this sheet

At-Home-While-Away Checklist · the home side of a calmer trip, in two modes.

Do the door walk the night before

Ticks on the door walk get done twice: once the night before and once on leave day. Both crossings take 10 minutes each and the twice version is the one that actually catches things.

Hand the list to a helper

The "while you're away" rows ask very little — a neighbor with a curb day and a walk-by is plenty. Print a copy for them with only their rows highlighted; being asked for less makes more people say yes.

The return rows matter most

Nobody books the re-entry. Water back to full flow, thermostat back to normal, one easy dinner, and a laundry-free first hour — this stops your calm metabolism from turning arrivals into chores.

Shred the personal pages

Anything with contact info or gate codes gets shredded after the trip. Same rule as the medications card: nothing personal lingers in a drawer for years.
